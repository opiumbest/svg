About Brightpath

Brightpath was started by a small group of teachers who wanted a place to send students for extra practice that didn't bury the lesson under pop-ups and sign-up walls.

Every lesson is written to be read in under ten minutes and reviewed by a working teacher before it goes up.

We don't ask for an email address, we don't track what you read, and we don't sell anything. The site is paid for by donations and a few small school grants.

If something is confusing, that's a problem with the lesson, not with you.

Frequently asked questions

Is Brightpath really free?

Yes. There are no paid tiers, no trials and no locked lessons.

Do I need an account?

No. Open a lesson and start reading.

Who writes the lessons?

Current and former classroom teachers, with a review pass before publishing.

Can I use the lessons in my class?

Yes. Print them, share them or assign them as homework.

How often is new content added?

A few new lessons go up every week during the school year.
